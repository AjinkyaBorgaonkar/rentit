The fee was never the problem. Trusting a stranger costs more than the rental.

Four steps, and only one of them is a handshake.

The rest is a record. Every rental produces a condition passport that both sides sign, that neither side can edit, and that decides what happens if something breaks.

01

Verify

Aadhaar eKYC and a liveness selfie for both sides, before a price is agreed. High-risk pincodes are blocked outright.

02

Capture

Nine angles, the serial number and a function test, timestamped at listing. This is the baseline everything is measured against.

03

Hand over

A matching capture at the door, a tamper seal on the case, and one condition report signed by both parties on one screen.

04

Compare

The same nine angles within 24 hours of return. A line-by-line diff, a wear tolerance, and the deposit released at a timestamp.

  1. 01

    Identity before price

    Both sides pass eKYC and liveness before a rate is shown. A banned account cannot re-enter with a new number.

  2. 02

    Condition photographed, not described

    Nine angles and the serial number, shot the same way at listing, handover and return. The comparison is arithmetic, not opinion.

  3. 03

    Two signatures, one record

    The renter and the owner sign the same condition report on their own phones. Neither copy can be edited afterwards.

  4. 04

    A fund, with written exclusions

    Cover is capped, deductible, and excluded for off-platform deals, prohibited categories and unverified handovers. The exclusions are published, not discovered.

What the cover does and does not pay for

Your camera earns between shoots. Your drill earns between shelves.

RENTit is not a listing site with a payout button. We photograph the item, grade it, price it inside a guardrail, sit at the handover, and take the argument off your hands. The commission pays for that work — and we show you the whole calculation, including the two costs owners usually forget.

One cluster beats one country.

Rental only works at density. Three thousand households inside a 3 km ring means a match in minutes, a handover on foot, and a hub that pays for itself. We do not open cluster two until cluster one clears ₹2.5 lakh a year.

The questions people actually ask.

Including the one about whether any of this exists yet. It doesn't — the pilot is 90 days old on paper, not in production.

No. This is the storefront for a 90-day pilot in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. There is no catalogue to browse and no app. The plan is 25 lenders, 60 listings and 100 orders, run on this site and WhatsApp with a part-time hub manager.

Verified identity, a refundable deposit held by RENTit, and ₹50,000 of cover per item. Theft is covered only with an FIR — which is why the deposit and the KYC both exist. A rental without a signed handover record is not covered at all.

Normal wear — scratches, minor cosmetic marks — is the owner's. Accidental damage comes from the protection fund, with a ₹2,500 deductible paid by the renter. Negligence and misuse are the renter's, at full repair cost. The matrix is written down and shown before booking.

It funds the parts that make a stranger safe: KYC, the nine-angle capture, the hub, the support line and the guarantee reserve. Verified power lenders pay 20%; casual and new listings pay 30% until their record earns the discount.

Self-pickup is the default, because delivery at ₹149 each way destroys the contribution on anything below ₹2,700. Delivery is a paid upgrade, free above ₹2,500 or for members at ₹199 a month.

Medical devices, weapons, hazardous chemicals, and high-risk tools without an induction. Anything without proof of ownership — an invoice, a serial number or the original box — stays off the network.
