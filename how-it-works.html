A handshake, turned into a file.

Every RENTit rental runs the same four steps, in the same order, whether the item is a ₹500 drill or a ₹65,000 camera. That repetition is the product. It is what makes a stranger's word unnecessary.

03HandoverMatching capture, tamper seal, two signatures
04ReturnSame nine angles, measured diff, deposit released
01 Before the rental · the baseline

A record is made before anyone asks for the keys.

The owner lists the item once. We shoot nine angles in a fixed order, read the serial number, run a function test and grade the condition. That file becomes the reference for every rental that follows — so the fifth renter is measured against the first, not against a memory.

The angle set never changes: front, back, both sides, top, bottom, mount, port side, and the serial plate. Same lights, same distance, same crop. A photograph that cannot be reproduced is not evidence.

02 At handover · both sides sign

Two phones, one record, the same eight minutes.

The renter meets the owner at the door or the hub counter. The second capture is shot in the same nine angles as the baseline, on the renter's phone. Both devices show the same diff, and both people sign the same report — not a PDF, not a WhatsApp message.

The case gets a tamper seal with a serial. If the seal is broken on return, the comparison starts from the seal, not from an argument about who opened it.

03 On return · the diff

The deposit is released by arithmetic, not by mood.

Within 24 hours of return, the same nine angles are shot again and compared zone by zone. Scratches that were there at the baseline read as zero. Anything new reads as a number, and the number decides whether it is wear or a claim.

The renter sees the diff before the owner does. If nothing is flagged, the deposit is released at a timestamp — usually the same evening, not "within five to seven working days".

Three grades, and a fourth answer.

Condition is not a feeling. Each grade has a written definition and photographed examples, so two people looking at the same scratch reach the same number.

A

9.0–10.0

As new. No visible wear, full function, original accessories. Required for cameras and lenses.

B

7.0–8.9

Light wear. Cosmetic marks only, everything works. Priced one band below an A item.

C

5.0–6.9

Visible wear. Scratches or scuffs, fully functional. Never listed as a premium camera.

—

Below 5.0

Not listed. Cracked optics, missing parts, a safety risk, or no proof of ownership.

Scores are the mean of nine zone readings. The grade is what the renter sees; the zone scores are what the diff uses.

If it goes wrong, the clock is published.

Most rental disputes are not disagreements about facts. They are disagreements about time — who has to answer, and by when. Ours are written down before the rental starts.

0–24 h

Claim window

Either side can open a claim within 24 hours of the signed return. After that, the record closes.

24 h

Acknowledged

A named person replies with the diff, the policy line and what happens next. No ticket number without a person.

72 h

Resolved or quoted

Either a decision, or a written repair quote with the deductible applied and the balance shown.

7 days

Paid

The fund pays the owner. The renter's deposit is released the moment no claim is open.
