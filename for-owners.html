The maths first. The pitch, if there's time.

Lending platforms talk about passive income. This page shows the statement: what the item earns, what RENTit keeps, what upkeep costs you whether it rents or not, and what is left.

Nothing here is a projection. The rates, the commission and the upkeep figures are the ones in our own model, including the two examples that fail.

75%of the rental is yours. RENTit keeps 25% to run verification, cover and the hub.
₹50,000cover per item per rental, with a ₹2,500 deductible on accidental damage.
48hfrom a signed return to money in your account. Not five to seven working days.

Set the rate. Watch what survives the month.

Owners are usually shown gross earnings, because gross is the number that sounds like income. This panel subtracts the commission, then subtracts maintenance and depreciation: the costs that arrive whether the item rents or sits on a shelf.

Defaults are the model's power-tool example: ₹800 a day, two days a month, ₹600 of upkeep. Change any of them.

Only list what clears the bar.

Our rule is 2% net monthly yield after commission, maintenance and depreciation. On the model's own numbers that clears a camera and a power tool, and fails a console and a sofa. We would rather say so before you photograph anything.

Where the money goes, and in what order.

Three facts decide whether lending feels worth it: when you get paid, what is deducted, and what happens when the item comes back broken.

Payout

48h

  • Signed return, then payment within 48 hours.
  • Deposit held by RENTit, never by you.
  • UPI or bank transfer, with a statement.

Commission

25%

  • Pays for eKYC on both sides.
  • Pays for the nine-angle capture and the hub.
  • Funds the ₹50,000 cover reserve.
  • 20% for verified power lenders, 30% for new ones.

₹3,600

  • − ₹1,800 commission.
  • − ₹1,800 maintenance and depreciation.
  • = ₹3,600 net, a 5.5% monthly yield.

Four steps to your first payout.

The pilot onboards by phone and at the counter, not through a signup flow. A hub manager walks the first listing with you; after that it is repeatable.

01

Show us the item

Photos, an invoice or serial number, and a two-minute call. Nothing gets listed without proof of ownership.

02

We grade it

Nine angles, a function test, and a written grade. Free professional photos for the listing.

03

Agree a rate

You set the rate inside a 2–5% of value guardrail. We show the net yield before you commit.

04

Hand it over

The renter arrives, the capture matches, both sign, and the payout starts on return.

The three objections, answered.

Payout, deposit, protection. Every owner asks in that order, and every owner is right to.
